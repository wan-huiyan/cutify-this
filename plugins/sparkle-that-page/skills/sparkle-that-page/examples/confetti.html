Every confetti

13 families, 3 palettes each. Click or tap a card and that confetti fires from where you clicked, in the motion chosen above. Every still picture is drawn by the same SparkleThatPage.clickConfetti.draw() the effect uses.

The two small panels on each card are a light page and a dark page: every palette has a version for each, and the outline switches with them. The big panel follows this page.

    Add one to your page: put the attributes from a card on <html> and load click_confetti.js. Fire one from your own code: SparkleThatPage.clickConfetti.burst(x, y, { family, palette, motion }).
