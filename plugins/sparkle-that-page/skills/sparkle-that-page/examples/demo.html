✨ sparkle-that-page

Cute, zero-dependency page effects. Flip the switches → watch the page come alive. Part of the cutify-this bundle.

/plugin install sparkle-that-page@wan-huiyan-cutify-this

✨ Toggle effects

cute-chrome is always on — select this text, hover the buttons, peek at the scrollbar & spinner below. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Move your mouse around ✦

Turn on Fairy cursor and Sparkle trail, then drift the pointer across the page. Click anywhere for a little burst.

Stamp it ✅

With Click confetti on, a button marked data-stamp lands a rubber-stamp label on itself and bursts confetti. The text is the attribute's value.

Things worth clicking glow ✨

With Fairy cursor on, hover a link, a button above, or this — the wand lights up over anything clickable and goes back to plain over text.

Scroll down to fill the meter 📜

Turn on the Scroll indicator (ring / comet / mascot) and scroll — it tracks how far down the page you are, and cheers (class is-end) when you reach the very bottom.

Switch tabs and come back 👀

With Tab-title peekaboo on, click away to another browser tab — the title (and favicon) turn into a little "come back! 🥺", then restore when you return.

A gentle snowfall of petals 🌸

Turn on Ambient floaters and pick sakura, bubbles, snow, or sparkle dust drifting quietly behind the content.

Sounds, a secret word and the sky 🔊

The round speaker at the bottom-left turns on tiny synthesized sounds (off until you press it): a chime for confetti and stamps, a soft tick on the celebrate button, a twinkle for the party below. Each palette has its own voice — try neon, then candy.

🤫 Psst — type the secret word magic anywhere on this page (not inside a text box) for a party. Change the word with data-secret on <html>.

The sun / moon button at the top-left flips light and dark, remembers your choice and never flashes the wrong theme on reload.
