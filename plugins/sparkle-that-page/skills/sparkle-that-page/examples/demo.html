✨ sparkle-that-page

Cute, zero-dependency page effects. Flip the switches → watch the page come alive. Part of the cutify-this bundle.

/plugin install sparkle-that-page@wan-huiyan-cutify-this

✨ Toggle effects

cute-chrome is always on — select this text, hover the buttons, peek at the scrollbar & spinner below. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Move your mouse around ✦

Turn on Fairy cursor and Sparkle trail, then drift the pointer across the page. Click anywhere for a little burst.

Scroll down to fill the meter 📜

Turn on the Scroll indicator (ring / comet / mascot) and scroll — it tracks how far down the page you are.

Switch tabs and come back 👀

With Tab-title peekaboo on, click away to another browser tab — the title (and favicon) turn into a little "come back! 🥺", then restore when you return.

A gentle snowfall of petals 🌸

Turn on Ambient floaters and pick sakura, bubbles, snow, or sparkle dust drifting quietly behind the content.
