Every scroll companion

Nine little companions that show how far down the page you are, plus the two classics. Click a card and that one joins this page: scroll slowly to see it move, flick fast to see it hold on, and reach the bottom to see it cheer.

Each card shows the four states the companion goes through, drawn by the same SparkleThatPage.scrollIndicator.svg() the live one uses, then the same four at their real size on a white page and on a dark one.

    Keep scrolling

    The companion you picked is travelling with you. A slow scroll gets the "moving" pose and a gentle sway. A quick flick of the wheel or a swipe gets "holding on": a worried face, wind lines and a little shiver, until you stop.

    None of this blocks a click: the companion and its dotted rail let every click pass through to the page underneath.

    Almost there

    Progress is honest: 0 at the top of the page, 1 at the very bottom. The climber's place on its rail, the rocket's height, the caterpillar's distance, the sprout's leaves and the moon's phase all read that one number.

    Try the dark switch at the top while you scroll: the companion repaints straight away, with a light outline that stays readable on a dark page.

    The end

    This is the bottom of the page. Your companion should be cheering now: arms up, sparkles, a happy face, and one little hop. Scroll up a bit and back down to see it again.

    Add one to your page: put the attributes from a card on <html> and load scroll_indicator.js. How to draw a new companion: references/scroll-mascots.md.
