Every floater

12 families, each drawn as shapes (no font characters), each with its own way of moving. Click a card and that family floats on this page, so you can watch how it moves. Every picture is drawn by the same SparkleThatPage.ambientFloaters.preview() the effect uses.

Each card shows the family on a light page (left) and a dark page (right). Use the switch above to turn this whole page dark and watch the floaters change colour as they fall.

Add one to your page: put data-floaters on <html> and load ambient_floaters.js. How to draw a new family: references/floaters.md.
