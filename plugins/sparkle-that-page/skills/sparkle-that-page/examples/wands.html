Every wand

11 families, 3 palettes each. Hover a card, or tab to it, and that wand becomes your cursor on this page. Every picture is drawn by the same SparkleThatPage.fairyCursor.svg() the cursor uses.

In each pair the left wand is the plain cursor and the right is its brighter twin, shown over links and buttons (try the toggle above).

    Add one to your page: put the two attributes from a card on <html> and load fairy_cursor.js. How to draw a new family: references/wands.md.
