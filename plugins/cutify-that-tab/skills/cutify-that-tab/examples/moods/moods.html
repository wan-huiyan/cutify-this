Tab icon moods

Each mascot awake and asleep, at 16 px in a tab and as a 64 px pixelated enlargement, on the four Chrome tab colours. Wiring and rules: favicon_mood.js, and the "Moods" section of SKILL.md.

This page's own tab icon

It sleeps between 18:00 and 07:00 and whenever you are in another tab. The buttons are a page-state override: CutifyTab.mood.set(...).

Awake and asleep

The sleep file keeps the starter's body and palette. The eyes become 3-cell curves and a small z appears, in the free air when there is some, otherwise on the body.

Page states: mochi cat, sad and starry

An error and a success. Set them from the page with CutifyTab.mood.set('sad') / set('starry'); set(null) hands the icon back to the clock.
